Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? expand_more
You can book an appointment through our website using the "Book Appointment" button, by calling our 24/7 helpline, or by visiting our hospital directly. We offer both physical and tele-consultations.
What are visiting hours? expand_more
Standard visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. However, for critical care units, visiting hours may vary and are restricted to immediate family members only.
Do you accept international insurance? expand_more
Yes, we partner with major international health insurance providers including Bupa, Allianz, and Aetna. Please check with our patient services desk for a full list of partners.
What specialized centers do you have? expand_more
We have Centers of Excellence in Cardiology, Oncology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Gastroenterology, and a dedicated Mother & Child wing.
